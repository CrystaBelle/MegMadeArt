# Meg Made Art — editing checklist

This is an adaptation of HTML5 UP's Prologue template. Keep `LICENSE.txt` and the footer credit when publishing under its Creative Commons Attribution license.

1. Open `index.html` in a text editor. Replace **all** instances of `artist@example.com` with Meg's email address. The links begin with `mailto:` and open a visitor's email app.
2. Replace the sample artwork titles, prices, media, sizes, and biography. The four abstract gallery images are AI-generated samples and are **not Meg's actual work**. Replace `images/art-1.jpg` through `images/art-4.jpg` with her photos, keeping the names or changing the paths in HTML. Update each image's `alt` description.
3. The flower photo in `images/hero-flower.jpg` came from the uploaded David Clode Unsplash file. Replace it if Meg wants one of her works featured instead. Check the source photo's licensing/attribution details before public use.
4. Exact palette: black `#000000`, red `#910013`, yellow `#EDC605`, white `#FFFFFF`. Edit `assets/css/meg-made-art.css` for layout/colors. Keep `assets/css/main.css` as the base Prologue styling.
5. Open `index.html` in a browser to preview. To use Replit, upload the contents of this folder so `index.html`, `assets/`, and `images/` are at the root. No AI credits or build step are needed for this static site.

No cart, checkout, or backend is included.
